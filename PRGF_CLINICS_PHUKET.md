## Suggested Questions to Ask When Contacting

1. Do you offer PRGF (Endoret) specifically, or standard PRP? What system/centrifuge do you use?
2. How many sessions are included in a full treatment package?
3. What is the total package cost (including consultation, blood draw, and all sessions)?
4. What is the duration of the full treatment program (days/weeks)?
5. Is there a follow-up or maintenance session schedule?
6. What areas do you treat (face, hair, joints, dental)?
7. Are there any current promotions or package discounts?
